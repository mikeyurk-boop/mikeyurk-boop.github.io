Affordable 3D printing

At Michael's 3D Printing

At Michael's 3D Printing, we believe everyone should be able to enjoy the creativity of 3D printing without spending too much money. Our prices are based on the amount of filament, printing time, and work that goes into making each item.

Even though filament can be inexpensive, every print takes time, creativity, and effort. That's why we keep our prices fair and affordable while making sure every customer gets something they're happy with.
